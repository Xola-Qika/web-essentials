Our Story

4K Productions is a dynamic media production company established in 2023 by a group of five talented Film and Television students from the University of the Witwatersrand doing their final year: Thato Khalo, Thendo Muthivhi, Sifiso Sibiya, Xola Qika, and Thabo Mphuthi.

We specialize in crafting premium, visually engaging content that resonates with audiences and drives engagement across social media platforms. Our team blends academic filmmaking expertise with creative, trend-driven strategies to produce high-quality video and photography for brands across various industries.

Having collaborated with restaurants, aviation schools, lifestyle brands, and event-based services, we understand the importance of creating visually rich, shareable content that matches the unique personality of each business.

We combine cinematic technique with social media strategy, ensuring every frame aligns with your brand’s identity and engages your target audience. From concept to final edit, we tailor each production to your unique goals — whether it’s showcasing your product, highlighting your space, or amplifying your brand story. Our approach is creative, collaborative, and detail-driven — because we know that great video doesn’t just look good, it feels right.

MEET THE TEAM

Thato Khalo

Thato Khalo serves as the Chief Executive Officer of 4K Productions, leading the company’s strategic direction and executive decision-making. As the visionary behind the brand, he drives sustainable growth through strong leadership, business insight, and a results-oriented mindset. His ability to combine innovation with strategic planning ensures the company consistently achieves high-performing outcomes while maintaining a clear and focused long-term vision.

Sifiso Sibiya

Sifiso Sibiya is an Executive Director and Creative Director at 4K Productions, responsible for shaping and directing the company’s creative vision. He specializes in content creation and oversees projects from concept to execution, ensuring high-quality and impactful results. In addition to his creative leadership, he plays a key role in executive decision-making, contributing ideas and strategies that support the company’s continuous growth and innovation.

Thendo Muthivhi

Thendo Muthivhi, co-founder of 4K Productions, brings a strong creative and academic background with a Bachelor of Arts in Film and Television. With over seven years of industry experience, he specializes in cinematography and video editing, producing visually compelling and engaging content. His attention to detail and technical expertise play a vital role in maintaining the company’s high production standards and creative excellence.

Thabo Mphuthi

Thabo Mphuthi is the Chief Operating Officer of 4K Productions, overseeing operational strategy, workflow management, and overall production efficiency. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Film and Television from the University of the Witwatersrand, where he developed strong technical and storytelling skills. His leadership ensures smooth coordination across all departments, allowing the company to consistently deliver high-quality projects on time.

Xola Qika

Xola Qika is the Chief Technology Officer at 4K Productions, leading the company’s technological and digital initiatives with a forward-thinking approach. He specializes in web development, sound design, and audio engineering, combining both creative and technical expertise. With a Bachelor of Arts in Film and Television from the University of the Witwatersrand, he plays a key role in strengthening the company’s digital presence and enhancing overall production capabilities.
